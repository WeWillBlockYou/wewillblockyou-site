Good to know

Tips for a Good Match

⬜
Plain background
A white desk works for dark parts. A dark mat works for white and light grey ones.
🔎
Show the tricky bit
Turn the part so its clip, hole or underside is in view. That's often what tells two parts apart.
🎨
Check the colour
The colour is a guess. Phone cameras can mix up greys and tans, so double-check before you buy.
📸
Add another angle
Up to 3 photos of the same item gives a better match. Try again from a new angle if the first result looks wrong.
